About NASA-PEER

the first post-baccalaureate constellation mentorship network at NASA

The Vision

As an increasing number of students are applying for graduate school each year without a step-wise increase in the number of available seats for admission, more and more students are taking post-bachelor research internships or related employment. The purpose of NASA-PEER is to strengthen the STEM pipeline by developing programming and mentorship networks for these early career researchers.

NASA-PEER connects post-bacs with research opportunities at NASA GSFC. Beyond this, NASA-PEER provides support, community, and skill-building via targeted programming and a strong constellation mentoring network. The mentoring network provides early career researchers at GSFC with meaningful leadership and mentorship opportunities.
